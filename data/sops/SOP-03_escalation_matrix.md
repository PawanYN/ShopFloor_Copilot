# SOP-03 Escalation Matrix

## 3.1 When to escalate

Escalate to a person, and do not decide alone, in any of these cases: a crack of any length; any proposal to scrap a part; a part ID that cannot be read; two procedures that give different instructions for the same case; a measurement within 0.5 mm of a tolerance limit; or an inspection result the system reports with low confidence.

## 3.3 Who handles which case

Cracks and any question about a tolerance limit go to the Quality Lead on shift. Scrap approvals and work-order holds go to the Shift Supervisor on shift. Camera, lighting or fixture faults at the inspection station go to the Maintenance Engineer. Conflicting procedures go to the Quality Lead, who informs the Plant Quality Manager.

## 3.4 Response times

The Quality Lead must respond to a crack escalation within 15 minutes. The Shift Supervisor must respond to a scrap approval request within 30 minutes. If there is no response in that time, escalate to the Plant Quality Manager.

## 3.5 Shift hours

The day shift runs from 06:00 to 18:00. The night shift runs from 18:00 to 06:00. Use the contact listed for the shift that is running at the time of the escalation.

## 3.6 Priority work orders

When a work order is marked as priority for a customer-critical delivery, any defect that leads to rework or scrap must also be reported directly to the Plant Quality Manager, in addition to the normal escalation.
