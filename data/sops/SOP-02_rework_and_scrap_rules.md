# SOP-02 Rework and Scrap Decision Rules

## 2.1 Principle

A part is reworked only when the defect can be removed without taking the part outside its dimensional tolerance. When that is not possible, the part is proposed for scrap. Rework and scrap decisions are recorded against the work order.

## 2.2 Reworking surface scratches

A scratch longer than 10 mm and up to 20 mm long may be removed by polishing. A scratch longer than 20 mm must be scrapped, because polishing to that depth thins the plate below its minimum thickness.

## 2.3 Cracks

A cracked part is never reworked. Welding, filling or grinding a crack is forbidden. After the Quality Lead has reviewed the part, it is proposed for scrap.

## 2.4 Reworking dimensional deviations

An oversize part may be re-machined once to bring it inside tolerance, provided it is oversize by no more than 2.0 mm. An undersize part cannot be corrected and is proposed for scrap.

## 2.5 Rework limit

A part may be reworked one time only. If the part fails inspection again after rework, it is proposed for scrap. The rework count is recorded on the work order.

## 2.6 Scrap authorisation

Scrap is irreversible. A line operator or an automated system may only propose scrap. The Shift Supervisor on duty must approve every scrap decision before the part is removed from the line and the work order is updated.
