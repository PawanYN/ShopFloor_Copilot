# SOP-01 Visual Tolerance Specification

## 1.1 Scope

This specification applies to bracket plate BP-120, machined from aluminium, with a nominal size of 120.0 mm wide and 80.0 mm high. It sets the acceptance limits for defects found at the visual inspection station. All limits are in millimetres.

## 1.2 Surface scratch limits

A surface scratch is a straight, shallow mark that does not break the material. A scratch up to 10 mm long is acceptable and the part ships as it is. A scratch longer than 10 mm and up to 25 mm long must be reworked by polishing. A scratch longer than 25 mm is not acceptable and the part goes to scrap review.

## 1.3 Crack limits

A crack is a jagged or branching line where the material has separated. No crack is acceptable at any length. A part with a crack must be placed on hold immediately and escalated to the Quality Lead. A cracked part is never shipped and never reworked.

## 1.4 Dimensional tolerance

The width must be 120.0 mm plus or minus 1.0 mm, and the height must be 80.0 mm plus or minus 1.0 mm. A part inside this tolerance is acceptable. A part that is oversize by more than 1.0 mm and up to 2.0 mm may be reworked by re-machining. A part that is undersize by more than 1.0 mm, or off by more than 2.0 mm in either direction, is not acceptable and goes to scrap review.

## 1.5 Tolerance summary table

| Defect type | Measured value | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Surface scratch | length up to 10 mm | Accept |
| Surface scratch | length over 10 mm, up to 25 mm | Rework by polishing |
| Surface scratch | length over 25 mm | Scrap review |
| Crack | any length | Hold and escalate to Quality Lead |
| Dimensional deviation | within 1.0 mm of nominal | Accept |
| Dimensional deviation | oversize by over 1.0 mm, up to 2.0 mm | Rework by re-machining |
| Dimensional deviation | undersize by over 1.0 mm, or off by over 2.0 mm | Scrap review |

## 1.6 Measurement uncertainty

The inspection camera measures size and length with an uncertainty of 0.5 mm. When a measured value is within 0.5 mm of a limit in this specification, the camera reading alone must not be used. The operator must confirm the value with a calibrated caliper before the part is accepted, reworked or sent to scrap review.
