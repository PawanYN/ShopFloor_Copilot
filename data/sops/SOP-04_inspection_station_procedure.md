# SOP-04 Inspection Station Procedure

## 4.1 Positioning the part

Place one part flat on the dark fixture plate, with the stamped ID at the bottom left and facing the camera. The part must sit fully inside the marked frame. Do not stack parts or place two parts in one photo.

## 4.2 Camera and lighting check

The camera is fixed 300 mm above the fixture and must not be moved. At the start of every shift, photograph the calibration target and confirm that the station reports the target size correctly. Switch on both light bars and wait one minute before the first photo.

## 4.3 Cleaning before the photo

Wipe coolant, oil and swarf off the part with a lint-free cloth before taking the photo. Oil streaks and coolant lines can be mistaken for scratches.

## 4.4 Taking the photo

Take one photo per part. If the photo is blurred, or the part moved, take it again. Do not submit a photo in which a hand, a tool or a shadow covers the part.

## 4.5 When the ID cannot be read

If the system reports that the part ID is unreadable, read the stamped ID by eye and enter the work order number by hand. Never guess a digit. If the stamp itself is damaged or illegible, place the part on hold and escalate.

## 4.6 Calibration failure

If the calibration target is reported at the wrong size, stop using the station. Parts must not be accepted or rejected on size until the Maintenance Engineer has restored the calibration. Scratch and crack checks may continue by eye.
